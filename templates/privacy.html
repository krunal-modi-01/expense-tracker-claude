Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Spendly. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it. By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the service. This may include:

  • Account information such as your name and email address.
  • Expense records you create, including amounts, categories, and dates.
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited and actions taken within the app.
  • Device and browser information for security and compatibility purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to deliver and improve the Spendly experience:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To display and organise your expense records.
  • To send essential service-related communications (e.g. account alerts).
  • To identify and fix bugs or performance issues.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating this service.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely and we apply industry-standard safeguards to protect it from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. These include:

  • Encrypted storage of sensitive information such as passwords.
  • Restricted access to user data within our systems.
  • Regular reviews of our data collection and storage practices.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

5. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. We may use trusted third-party services to support core functionality (such as hosting infrastructure), and these providers are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and only as directed by us.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Spendly may use session cookies to keep you logged in and maintain your preferences. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics tools that monitor your behaviour across other websites. Any cookies used are strictly necessary for the application to function correctly.

7. User Rights

You have the right to control your data at any time. Specifically, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent to data processing where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
